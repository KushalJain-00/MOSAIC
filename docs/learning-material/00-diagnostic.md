# Diagnostic — First Socratic placement

**Tutor instruction:** ask question 1 only, wait for the learner's answer, and continue one at a time. Do **not** supply explanations before the diagnostic ends. After 3–5 questions, create the skill map and let the learner choose a focus.

1. **Probability:** two independent fair coins are tossed. What is the probability of at least one head, and how do you justify it without listing all outcomes?
2. **Expected value and costs:** a strategy makes +₹120 on 45% of trades and loses ₹80 on 55%. Before fees, should it be deployed? Why is your answer incomplete?
3. **Indian derivatives:** two NIFTY call contracts have the same strike but different expiries. Why must MOSAIC treat them as different instruments?
4. **Time-series integrity:** your model uses the final closing price to trigger a trade one minute before the session closes. What must you verify to exclude lookahead?
5. **Engineering:** a Parquet file has been finalized, but the application crashes before recording it in SQLite. How could the next run avoid claiming a duplicate published dataset?
